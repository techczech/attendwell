## Description (limit 4000)

AttendWell is the door screen for workshop check-in. Participants find their name on the iPad, tap it and confirm. The organiser sees who has arrived as it happens.

The iPad app works with the free AttendWell organiser app for Mac and Windows, which holds your workshops and participant lists. You need that app on a computer to use the iPad app with a real workshop. To see how check-in works without one, tap Try a demo on the first screen. It uses made-up names.

How it works

1. On your computer, create a workshop in the organiser app and paste or import the participant list.
2. Choose Start check-in. The organiser app opens its own connection, which can take up to a minute.
3. In the organiser app, choose Pair an iPad. On the iPad, scan the QR code.
4. Participants tap their name and confirm.

If the connection drops, the iPad keeps the check-ins and sends them when it returns.

What you need

- The AttendWell organiser app on a Mac or Windows computer with an internet connection. Download it from https://techczech.github.io/attendwell/
- An iPad.

Your data

Participant names stay on the organiser's computer. The iPad holds the names of the current workshop while it is paired. The apps have no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sent to the author. The camera is used only to scan the pairing code.

AttendWell is free and open source under the MIT licence. Made by Dominik Lukeš.

## App Review notes

AttendWell for iPad is the door screen for a desktop organiser app. The organiser runs the free AttendWell app on a Mac or Windows computer, and the iPad pairs with it. The app needs no account or sign-in.

To see the full check-in flow without a computer: on the first screen, tap Try a demo. It runs with made-up names and needs no network.

To test real pairing with the free Mac app:

1. Download AttendWell for Mac from https://techczech.github.io/attendwell/ and open it.
2. Create a workshop.
3. Paste in a few names, one per line.
4. Choose Start check-in.
5. Wait up to a minute for the connection to open. The organiser app opens it itself; no Cloudflare account or configuration is needed.
6. Choose Pair an iPad. A QR code appears.
7. On the iPad, scan the code with the camera. The camera is used only for this and no images are stored.
8. Tap a name on the iPad and confirm. The check-in appears in the organiser app.

Some venue and office networks block the connection. If it does not open, try another network.
