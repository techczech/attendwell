AttendWell

AttendWell is a workshop check-in app. Participants tap their name on an iPad at the door, and the organiser sees who has arrived on a Mac or Windows computer as it happens.

Who it is for

People who run workshops, training sessions and small events, and want a register that participants fill in themselves when they arrive.

How a check-in works

  1. On your computer, create a workshop in the AttendWell organiser app and paste or import the participant list.
  2. Start check-in. The organiser app opens its own connection and shows a QR code.
  3. On the iPad, open AttendWell and scan the code to pair it with your computer.
  4. Participants tap their name and confirm. You see each arrival in the organiser app straight away. If the connection drops, the iPad keeps the check-ins and sends them when it returns.

Downloads

  • AttendWell for Mac — the organiser app, signed and notarised.
  • AttendWell for Windows — the organiser app. The installer is unsigned, so Windows shows a warning the first time you run it.
  • AttendWell for iPad — Coming soon to the App Store. The door app is free. It has a Try a demo option with made-up names, so you can see the check-in screens without an organiser app.

What you need

  • A Mac or Windows laptop with an internet connection, running the organiser app.
  • An iPad for the door.

Your data

Participant names stay on your computer. The iPad holds the names of the current workshop while it is paired. Nothing is sent to the author or to any server of his, and there are no accounts, analytics or tracking. The privacy page has the details. If something does not work, see support.
